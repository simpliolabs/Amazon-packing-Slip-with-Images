feat(title): Manual Title Scorer — type/score/push your OWN title (control when the council wobbles)

PO directive: the GPT-5 title council is stochastic (good one regen, regressed the next). The reliable
answer is to give the seller the final word — type the exact title, see its REAL score + Amazon-rule
check, and push it. Pairs with the 7-day cooling-lock (push a title you're happy with -> it's locked).

- /score-title endpoint (already added): runs the EXACT scoreListingContent (typed title substituted
  over live content) + validateTitle -> { titleScore/25, overallScore, ruleProblems, suppressionRisk }.
  Read-only, no migration, no Amazon write.
- push-content `title_override`: a manual title broadcasts the typed string to every SKU (children +
  parent + live FBM twins), capped at 200 chars, bypassing the AI recommendation.
- UI: the Ship-Title modal now has an editable textarea (seeded with the AI title), a "Check score +
  Amazon rules" button -> score + 🚫 suppression/rule violations, and "Push this title to all variants".

Adversarial pre-ship review caught + FIXED a BLOCKER on the live write path:
- CAPACITY FAMILIES: the override broadcast ONE typed title to every per-GB SKU -> would stamp "128GB"
  onto the 32GB SKU's live title, defeating per-child capacity titles, and it fired on the normal
  Confirm flow (editTitle seeded from the first child's title). Fixed at BOTH layers: server-side
  loadDiff IGNORES title_override when per_child_titles.length > 1 (authoritative); client-side the
  editor only seeds/renders for broadcast-title products. Also fixed: the Confirm button was gated on
  the AI diff (changed===0) so a typed title couldn't push when the AI title already matched — now
  gated on the typed title being non-empty.
