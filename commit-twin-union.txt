Presence flags: OR across FBA+FBM twin rows (kill the divergent-twin lottery)

Caught during the post-merge live verification of #186 (PO: "verify all claims").
The deployed flags were real but computed against ONE of the ASIN's twin rows -
and loadListingContentForPresence picked the most-populated row, which for
B0FK951X67 was the FBM twin carrying a STALE title ("...Comfort Colors Shirt ...
Blue Spruce"). The FBA row's actual title ("THE CEO I Could Be Meaner Tshirt
Comfort Colors Graphic Tee for Men & Women") was shadowed, so "comfort colors
tshirt women" (36,635/mo) still showed no T flag while "comfort colors blue
spruce" did. A twin lottery whenever the rows diverge - exactly the pre-push
state every un-optimized family is in.

Fix - correct OR semantics, not concatenation:
- checkPresenceAny(keyword, listings[]): checks each row INDIVIDUALLY and ORs
  the four flags; multiplier re-derived from the OR'd flags with the same
  ladder. Deliberately NOT a concatenated-text check: half a phrase in row A
  plus half in row B must not fake a match neither row has (adversarial
  self-catch - the first draft concatenated).
- loadListingRowsForPresence returns ALL twin rows; runKeywordEngine accepts
  ListingContent | ListingContent[] and ORs when given rows (both sync call
  sites pass rows now).
- intelligence GET live recompute uses checkPresenceAny over the rows.
- loadRepresentativeListingRow (most-populated single row) kept for the
  research title seed, which must be ONE title.

Once a broadcast push converges the twins, OR-of-rows equals either row - this
only matters for the diverged window, which is exactly when the seller is
looking at the flags.

tsc exit 0. No migration.
