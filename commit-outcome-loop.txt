Outcome loop (#89): per-keyword SQP share time-series → next-regen signal

The SQP-primary outcome loop, full sweep. Captures per-keyword impression-share
over time, derives a rose/flat/fell signal anchored to content changes, feeds it
as a conservative tiebreak into title-candidate selection, and surfaces an honest
line in the rank panel. HONEST by construction: it reports correlation ("share
rose AFTER a change"), never causation, and shows/does NOTHING until ≥2 monthly
snapshots accrue.

WHAT CHANGED
1. CAPTURE — migration 023 (keyword_share_snapshots, one row per keyword per SQP
   data-month, UNIQUE asin,keyword,snapshot_date) + shareSnapshots.ts. syncKeyword-
   Data snapshots each report's normalized 0-100 share + a content fingerprint.
   SQP-only (inherited/JS rows carry share=0); captured ONLY on a FRESH fetch (a
   cache hit would mislabel a stale-month report — adversarial-review finding);
   best-effort (a missing table never breaks a sync).
2. SIGNAL — outcomeSignals.ts: rose/flat/fell per keyword, with the insufficient-
   data guard (<2 monthly snapshots OR <100 volume → no signal) and the non-content-
   bottleneck rule (content changed but share flat/fell → reviews/price/velocity,
   not more copy).
3. FEEDBACK — a conservative TIEBREAK in selectTitleCandidates: reinforce rising
   keywords, de-prioritize stalled ones. SECONDARY to opportunityScore — only
   reorders ties, never across opportunity tiers, never drops a keyword. Strict
   no-op when signals are empty (every case until history accrues).
4. UI — the rank panel shows a server-authored + sanitize()-clamped line
   ("Share rose to X% after your last content change" / "Share flat at X% despite
   your last change — rank now likely depends on reviews, price, and velocity").
   Renders nothing until history exists.

ADVERSARIAL REVIEW (3 reviewers + per-finding verifier) → 2 real findings, both
fixed before push:
- (med) month-boundary cache hit could mislabel a stale-month SQP report under a
  new-month snapshot_date → capture now only fires on a FRESH fetch.
- (low) non-deterministic sibling-content fallback could flip the fingerprint →
  the sibling query is now ordered (deterministic), so the fingerprint is stable.
