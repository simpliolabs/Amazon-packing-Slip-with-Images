fix(title): design-GROUND apparel titles + GPT-5 council + stop the scorer nagging clean titles

LIVE verification of the title council (PR #135) FAILED: it ran end-to-end but still produced
"THE CEO Later Gator Comfort Colors T-Shirt Vintage 90s Cool T Shirt Gator Shirts Later
Alligator Shirt for Men and Women" (shirt 4x, gator 3x, ungrounded "Vintage 90s"). Root cause
was NOT the council — it was the BRIEF: the title agent handed every persona a keyword
candidateList + hard mandates ("include AT LEAST 3 UPGRADE keywords") + a forced verbatim
mustInclude, AND validateTitle penalized the title for NOT carrying those keywords. Garbage in,
garbage out — the council can't be cleaner than its brief allows. "Vintage 90s" is a
high-search-volume keyword that is NOT on the design (a guess), pulled in purely by volume.

THE FIX — the TITLE is now design-LED, the rest of the listing stays keyword-led:
- Design-grounding gate (runTitleAgent, apparel only): builds a vocabulary from the design name +
  what the image scan literally read (visionDesign) + the garment brand + audience. A keyword may
  enter the TITLE only if every DISTINCTIVE word is on the design. Generic tone/structural/gift
  words ("cool", "funny", "gift", "tee") are always allowed, so good phrases ("alligator lover
  gift") survive while ungrounded CLAIMS ("vintage 90s", "retro") are dropped. The gate filters the
  candidate pool AND upgradeKws AND — critically — the forced verbatim mustInclude (the primary
  leak: it's mandated + hard-validated, bypassing the candidate filter). Nothing is lost: bullets +
  backend build their keyword pools independently and still rank every ungrounded term.
- Soft upgrade line for apparel: no more "MANDATORY #3 include AT LEAST 3" — design-led titles do
  not chase keyword coverage.
- GPT-5 on the council's adversary + judge (PO directive): the proposers stay on fast gpt-4.1-mini
  for diverse drafts; the two reasoning calls run on GPT-5 (TITLE_COUNCIL_MODEL || gpt-5) with the
  reasoning-model param shape (max_completion_tokens, no temperature, reasoning_effort low) and a 60s
  budget. Fails open to the SEO/anti-stuffing draft, not the creative one.
- Scorer (syncListingContent): waive the upgrade-keyword TITLE penalty for apparel. A clean
  design-led title was being docked -3/-5 for "missing" upgrade keywords and re-triggering the
  "ship" nag on an otherwise-perfect title — the exact 12/12-but-ship contradiction. Non-apparel
  (where specs like 256GB/UHS-I ARE title search terms) keeps the penalty.

Adversarial pre-ship review (standing directive) caught and FIXED two BLOCKERs before commit:
1. mustInclude bypassed the gate entirely (the verbatim money keyword is the PRIMARY stuffing
   channel) -> now grounding-gated. 2. The original all-words-grounded bar over-stripped good
   keywords and could strand a title under 80 chars -> switched to a distinctive-words-only bar
   (generic words always allowed). Also: GPT-5 reasoning could truncate the title to empty ->
   raised the token floor + low effort + fail-open to the lean draft. attributePin was reviewed and
   deliberately NOT gated (a garment brand is a product attribute, not a design claim — grounding is
   the wrong test). Grounding predicate unit-tested on the Later Gator keyword set before commit.
