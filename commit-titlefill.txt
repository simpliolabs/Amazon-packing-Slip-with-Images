Title-fill reliability: fill apparel titles to ~73-75 chars (was stalling at 65-70)

Batch item 3/6. PO 2026-06-15: "65/75, didn't use all chars" — clean apparel titles kept
landing at 65-70, leaving the 75-char budget under-used. The fill block's trigger (<70) and
break (>=70) capped it right where the PO saw the gap, and the filler pool ran dry when the
top title candidates were already token-covered.

Three surgical changes to the existing fill block:
- Trigger <70 -> <73 (kick in for clean 70-72 char titles too).
- Break >=70 -> >=73 (keep adding until the title reliably approaches the cap).
- Filler pool now includes the high-value UPGRADE keywords (topUpgradeKws) alongside the
  title candidates + canonical bigrams, so there's enough GROUNDED material to reach the
  target instead of starving.

All existing safety preserved per-addition: the 75-char hard cap (next+tail > 75 -> skip),
the token-novelty check (adds nothing new -> skip), the gender-lean filter, and the
motif/garment truthfulness strips run on every candidate. topUpgradeKws is already
grounding-filtered and trademark-safe (#240 scrubs the published output too). A fill can
still never smuggle back what a guard removed.

tsc 0. node_modules has no vitest in this sandbox so pipeline.test.ts could not run, but the
change is a contained numeric-threshold + filler-source edit with every guard intact.
