fix(title): apparel titles follow the IDEAL design-led structure (brand -> design -> product type)

Live verify of the design-grounding fix removed "Vintage 90s" + the keyword dump (good), but the
STRUCTURE was still wrong vs the ideal we designed: it came out "THE CEO Later Gator for Comfort
Colors Graphic T-Shirt Gator Shirt for Men and Women" — the blank brand jammed in BEFORE the
product type, with an awkward "for". The ideal is "THE CEO Later Gator T-Shirt ... for Men and
Women": brand -> design name -> product type FIRST, then grounded descriptors.

Root cause: I cleaned the keywords (grounding) but left the brief's RIGID mandates intact — "#1
keyword FRONT-LOADED", "#2 blank-brand right after the #1 keyword" — which shove the garment brand
between the design name and the product type. The Workflow demo that produced the clean ideal had
none of those pins; it was just "brand -> design -> product type -> audience, natural language".

Restructured the APPAREL brief to the ideal (non-apparel is unchanged — specs-led titles work):
- Order: explicit design-led template + a concrete skeleton for THIS product ("<brand> <design>
  <garment brand> T-Shirt, <one grounded descriptor> for <audience>"). LEAD with design + product
  type; do not bury them behind keywords.
- Garment/blank brand is an ADJECTIVE on the product type ("Comfort Colors Tee"), NEVER "for Comfort
  Colors" (it's the fabric brand, not a compatibility target — that "for" was the live wart).
- Money keyword: woven in naturally, no longer FRONT-LOADED ahead of the design name / product type.
- Front-load rule: apparel leads with brand + design + product type, not the keyword.
- Deterministic backstop: strip any stray "for <blank brand>" the LLM still emits (the audience
  "for Men and Women" is untouched) — belt-and-suspenders for the verified wart.

Will re-verify live on Later Gator: title should now read "THE CEO Later Gator [Comfort Colors]
T-Shirt ... for Men and Women" — design-led, no "for Comfort Colors", no stuffing.
