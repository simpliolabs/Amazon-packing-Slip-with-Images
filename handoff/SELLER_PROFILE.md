## 1. BRAND IDENTITY
- The brand is **THE CEO** (CEO® on packing slips). Every title **starts** with it — position 0, no exceptions (judge docks −20).
- The brand appears in the **title and brand attribute only**: never in description body prose (stripped, even inside quotes), never in backend keywords (the attribute already indexes it — the byte buys nothing).
- Garment-brand casing is authoritative from the spec table ("Comfort Colors", never "comfort colors").

## 2. GARMENT BLANKS & PRODUCT FACTS
- **Comfort Colors 1717** (confirmed): Relaxed fit, Short Sleeve, Crew Neck, midweight 6.1 oz garment-dyed, 100% Ring-Spun Cotton, Low Stretch, Runs Slightly Small. **The name IS a selling point** — it belongs in titles next to the money keyword.
- **Gildan 64000 Softstyle** (confirmed 2026-07-31): Classic fit, Short Sleeve, Crew Neck, lightweight 4.5 oz ring-spun. Material stated **without** a percentage (heathers are blends). **The name is NOT a selling point** — its facts feed copy, the word "Gildan" never appears in any customer-facing field. SKUs carry the style number (640002XL-…) as identification.
- **Spec beats search**: fit/weight/sleeve/neck/material come ONLY from the confirmed spec table, never inferred from keyword demand ("oversized" search volume does not make a relaxed tee oversized).
- **Weight truth**: pin the real fabric when the blank is known; claim NO weight when it isn't.
- **Production method**: garments are **printed**, never "screen-printed"/"silk-screened" (auto-rewritten to "printed").
- **Unisex truth**: the blanks are unisex — "women's fit"/"men's fit" fabricates a gender the garment doesn't have. Audience targeting ("for Women") is fine; fit-gendering is not.
- A new blank earns copy mentions only by being confirmed and added to the spec table (a PO confirmation like "Gildan 6400 - YES" is the trigger).
- **Mixed-blank families (PO decision 2026-08-04): family-level resolution STANDS.** The only mix we offer is Gildan 64000 + Bella+Canvas 3001, and they are near-identical (lightweight ring-spun cotton, short sleeve, crew neck); per-child blank resolution was considered and DECLINED as not worth the programming. First matching row over the family hay (Gildan, by id order) grounds the whole family. Revisit only if a genuinely different blank ever gets mixed into one family.
- **Gildan 64000 stretch/fit-to-size CONFIRMED 2026-08-04** (closes the §12 open): Low Stretch, True to Size — sourced from the official Gildan G64000 page ("Modern classic fit", 100% ring-spun cotton, no elastane) and applied to blank_specs by the PO. Rows also carry `source_url` provenance now.
- **The spec table lives in the DATABASE** (2026-08-04, migration 053): `blank_specs` in Supabase is the authoritative catalog — adding/correcting a blank is **one SQL INSERT/UPDATE, no deploy**; affected listings heal on their next regen (reader caches 5 min). The two rows above are seeded byte-identically; the same rows exist in code (`src/lib/fba/blankSpecs.ts`) only as a fail-open floor if the DB is unreachable. `brand_in_copy=false` encodes the Gildan rule; `match_pattern` is a case-insensitive regex over title/attributes/productType/SKUs (`\b64000` deliberately unclosed so "640002XL" matches). A fact-starved short ship now names its cure: SHIP_CENSUS title/description floor lines carry `factsGap:"blank.unknown"` when an apparel run matched no blank.

## 3. TITLES
- **GOLD (PO 2026-08-09, B0GVV3XL4T rewrite):** `THE CEO 2026 World Soccer Cup Tee Shirt | USA Mexico Canada Football Tee` (72) — PO's improvement on the AI's `…World Soccer Cup USA Mexico Canada Unisex Tee | Classic Fit`. **Teaches four rules:** (1) **MONEY KEYWORDS GO RIGHT OF THE PIPE** — the pipe-right is the money position; a spec FACT there ("Classic Fit") is a waste of the highest-value real estate. This RESOLVES the money-tail-vs-fact-tail open question: **the money tail WINS**. (2) Left of the pipe = brand + design identity + garment noun, nothing else. (3) **"Football" is a required international synonym on soccer products** — non-US shoppers search football, and "football tee" is its own demand. (4) "Unisex"/"Classic Fit" are NOT title words (they belong in bullets/description per the unisex rule §8) — the noun ×2 pattern (Tee Shirt … Tee) carries the garment instead.
- **GOLD (PO manual lock 2026-08-08, B0FKKN8XKV):** `THE CEO I Will Praise Him in Every Season Tee | Christian Shirts for Women` (~74/75) — brand + FULL design name + garment noun, pipe, then the top market-opportunity money keyword (7.0/10, 108K vol). The council should produce this shape unaided (#147 title half).
- **Hard law: ≤75 characters** (Amazon auto-rewrites longer, policy 2026-07-27). Golden band **70–75**. The push **refuses** a >75 title rather than truncating.
- **The 8 PO gold titles (2026-07-22) are the authority.** Canonical: `THE CEO See You Later Alligator Shirt | Long Sleeve Comfort Colors Shirt` (72). Golds win over any legacy rule. Protected as test fixtures — no net may alter them. **RECONCILED 2026-08-09 by the B0GVV3XL4T gold (PO ruling — supersedes the conservative placeholder):** the pipe-right is the MONEY position. A tail carrying the **garment BRAND** (`| Long Sleeve Comfort Colors Shirt` — gold #2; §2: the Comfort Colors name IS a selling point) stays PROTECTED. A tail carrying only **spec FACTS** (`| Classic Fit`, `| Unisex Tee`, a bare sleeve/fit/weight phrase) is **REPLACEABLE by the top market-opportunity keyword** — facts belong in bullets/description, never in the highest-value real estate. `enforceMoneyTail`'s `fact-tail` skip therefore narrows to BRAND-carrying tails only.
- **Pattern A** (design-led, pipe ` | `, 7 of 8 golds since 2026-08-09 — gold #5 rewritten from the obsolete inverted Pattern-B Christian form to the B0FKKN8XKV money-tail lock): `THE CEO [Design Phrase] [Noun] | [Attr] [Category Brand] [Noun-variant] [for Audience?]` — the pipe-right may instead be the ONE category MONEY-KEYWORD tail (`… Season Tee | Christian Shirts for Women`). Zero commas, zero dashes.
- **Pattern B** (front-load, for low-search designs): major category keywords first, design phrase LAST — e.g. `THE CEO Espana Championship Tee Shirt 2026 Spain Jersey Football Soccer Cup`. (The former Christian Pattern-B example — `THE CEO Christian Tee Shirt Comfort Color I Will Praise Him in Every Season` — was retired 2026-08-09 as obsolete: the PO's B0FKKN8XKV lock shows faith designs take the Pattern-A money tail instead.)
- **Product noun ×2 with variety**: two DISTINCT surface forms ("Tee Shirt … Tshirt", "Cap … Hat") — binding decision: variety, exactly two, never fold distinct nouns to one; only exact repeats ("Tshirt, Tshirt") are violations.
- **No modifier stuffing**: Funny/Novelty/Retro/Cute/Vintage/standalone-Graphic/Farewell/Goodbye banned as decorators. Attribute pairs the PO uses are fine ("Graphic Shirt", "Bold Motivational", "Puff Embroidery").
- **Idiom expansion** (contextual): "Later Gator" → "See You Later Alligator" when characters allow.
- **Design-led**: a keyword enters the title only when grounded in the actual design; titles are product claims. Pads come only from facts — never pool terms, never adjectives. Facts exhausted → honest shorter title. **EXCEPTION (PO-sanctioned by the B0FKKN8XKV lock, gold #5)**: the ONE pipe-right MONEY-TAIL slot may carry a category keyphrase from the target set (`enforceMoneyTail`, flag TITLE_MONEY_TAIL). SCOPE (settled by the 2026-08-09 ruling): the net only ever REPLACES an existing tail (a pipe-right or a bare trailing audience tail — never appends to a tail-less title), and never touches a pipe-right carrying **the design** (`design-right` — including the design's §6 identity synonyms, so the soccer gold's `| … Football Tee` is design vocabulary, not a churnable keyword) or **the garment brand** (`brand-tail`). A pure spec-fact tail is fair game.
- **ANSWERED 2026-08-09 (was the money-tail scope open question)**: may a money keyword replace an in-band gold-shape tail? **BRAND tail — no** (`brand-tail`, protected by `ctx.garmentBrand` AND a standing brand lexicon so gold #2 holds even when the blank row fails to match). **Pure SPEC-FACT tail — yes, no value floor** (`| Classic Fit`, `| Unisex Tee`, a bare sleeve/fit/weight phrase): the pipe-right is the money position. Implemented in `enforceMoneyTail` (src/lib/fba/titleBand.ts) — the old `fact-tail` decision is renamed `brand-tail` and the spec half is deleted. Still pending: keyword rows carrying celebrity names are scrubbed at generation + push + money-tail derivation, but the stored rows are NOT yet ratcheted IRRELEVANT (see §6).
- **TITLE WASTE VOCABULARY (PO ruling 2026-08-09)**: "Unisex" and "Classic Fit" are **not title words** — TITLE-only, the facts themselves are untouched in bullets/description/Item Highlights/Product Details. Enforced as a deterministic net on the shipped bytes at the shared `bandTitle` seam (`stripTitleWasteVocabulary`, src/lib/fba/titleBand.ts), running BEFORE the money tail so the freed characters reach the keyword. The PO's own guard is the gate: strip **only** when the removal frees space for a money keyword **or** the title still lands 70-75 after the facts pad; otherwise refuse, byte-identical. The facts pad is barred from re-adding the same vocabulary (one predicate, both ends), or a Gildan family would oscillate inside a single pass.
- **Noun ×2 is protected from the dedupe (2026-08-09)**: `collapseRepeatedWords` deduped significant words globally and was therefore deleting the second garment noun from gold #1 (72→66) and gold #3 (72→68) — the golds spend the noun once per side of the pipe. Garment-family words are now deduped PER ` | ` SEGMENT (max two); the #148 defect (a repeat inside ONE segment, "Tshirt, Tshirt") is unchanged.
- Title carries **1–2 keywords max**; no variant attributes (size/color); seasonal terms only when the design IS the occasion.
- Title-Case; existing uppercase (THE CEO, TShirt) preserved.
- **A PO-locked title is the spec** — never regenerated, survives every audit; risky (trademark) locked titles get a warning banner, never a hard block.

## 4. AUDIENCE & GENDER
- Lean set (male/female/lean_male/lean_female) → the title MUST carry the matching tail ("for Women" / "Women's" / "Ladies"). "for Men and Women" is **never** correct on a leaned listing.
- Universal design → audience tail is OPTIONAL; never force gender; a product keyword outranks a generic audience phrase in the 75-char budget.
- **Closed relational lexicons** (PO verbatim): FEMALE carriers = {wife, girlfriend}; MALE = {husband, boyfriend}. Bare pronouns are deliberately NOT carriers ("He's Golfing" is a women's shirt).
- **Hard lean** drops opposite-gender keywords everywhere (backend included); **soft lean** keeps both (cross-gender gift traffic is real).
- **Widow format**: "{Hobby} Widow/Wife" designs — the wearer is the SPOUSE, not the enthusiast. Never "for golf-loving women"; correct: "for the golf widow whose husband is always at the course". These spouse compounds ("golf widow shirt") are the money niche and get seeded deliberately.
- A gift design naming the recipient ("Best Husband Ever" on a ladies cut) blocks the opposite tail.
- A gender word appears at most ONCE in a title.
- **"for Men and Women" is a CHARACTER WASTE, not a feature (PO ruling 2026-08-09, on their own locked `THE CEO World Soccer Cup Soccer T-Shirt, Women'S T-Shirts for Men and Women` — verbatim: "is TERRIBLE and Wasting MEN, WOMEN")**: the inclusive tail eats ~20 of 75 chars that a money keyword should own. NEVER pair it with a gendered noun ("Women's … for Men and Women" is self-contradicting AND redundant). Universal design → prefer the top market-opportunity keyword in that space; use the audience tail only when nothing better fits. Also: casing artifacts like "Women'S" are defects — Title-Case must not capitalize the letter after an apostrophe.

## 5. KEYWORD PLACEMENT & BACKEND
- **Doctrine: backend is the overflow home.** Title = 1–2 money keywords; bullets = clean shopper prose (keywords only when natural, NEVER forced — bullets carry no coverage duty); description = facts + story; **backend = everything else** (synonyms, occasions, long-tail, misspellings). Coverage anywhere counts everywhere.
- Never duplicate a keyword in title AND backend (Amazon indexes a token once).
- **Backend bands**: 250-byte cap, fill to **240–250** ("why aren't we using the full 250?"), 220 strict floor, <190 = garbage. Per-color shade tails ~17 bytes on colored families (shared core 233). CRITICAL money keywords claim bytes first and are exempt from title-echo dedup.
- Banned backend filler: apparel/clothing/clothes/outfit/wear/fashion/trendy/stylish ("a promotional string, not keywords").
- **"Oversized" nuance**: never in visible copy (blank is Relaxed) — but allowed in backend (invisible indexing of real 636K/mo demand is not a claim).
- **Colors**: shared title/bullets carry NO color word; colors rank per-child via each child's own backend tail.
- **Seasonal**: strip only OFF-season terms — a Valentine design KEEPS "valentine" everywhere (its theme IS the holiday).
- **30 ranking-target seats**, one per search intent (no spelling-variant waste); theme-fit rated against a persisted PO-editable theme card; a thin niche design's own keywords are harvested and seat-reserved even at low volume.
- The 158→240 byte gap on thin designs is a **pool problem** — never "solved" by padding junk.
- **Opportunity score = Jungle Scout's MARKET metric, never our fabricated composite** (PO 2026-08-08: "the Opp score should be a JUNGLE SCOUT opp score, NOT our fabricated one — it should not be changing from pull to pull and be used to make the right keyword placement decisions"). The displayed/decision-driving opp number must be the provider's market opportunity (stable across pulls unless the MARKET moves); the internal volume×usage-gap composite may exist only as a clearly-named internal gap signal, never labeled "opportunity" and never the placement decider.
- **Easy wins are never ignored (PO 2026-08-08: "we shouldn't ignore cases like cute christian shirts for women — 6.2 and super easy to rank")**: HIGH ease_of_ranking + decent market opportunity = a priority placement/target candidate even at modest volume — the winnable battles for a niche catalog. Ease must be a REAL decision input (as of the trace it influenced nothing); volume-first ordering must not bury ease-100 keywords.
- **Targeting weighs ALL THREE factors explicitly (PO 2026-08-08: "We should look at all 3 Factors + Market opp + Ease + Volume to determine what to target")**: volume = size of the prize, ease = winnability, market_opportunity = the blend. No single-factor ordering; the target selector and placement decisions must consider the triple, with sanity floors so ease alone can't promote near-zero-volume junk.
- **Blank-brand placement waterfall (PO 2026-08-08: "if Comfort Colors is not in Title it needs to go to HIGHLIGHTS")**: the blank brand (Comfort Colors — only brands with brand_in_copy allowed; NEVER Gildan) must appear in the TITLE first; when the final title (incl. PO manual locks) lacks it, the Item Highlights MUST carry it as a deterministic fact. Enforce as a terminal net on the shipped IH bytes, not a prompt hint; runs on every title change including manual locks. IMPLEMENTED 2026-08-08: net = `ensureBlankBrandInHighlights` (src/lib/fba/blankSpecs.ts) — prepends `authentic <brand> blank` (prepend = survival through `capItemHighlightRepeats`' tail-drop at generation AND the push boundary), Gildan blocked by `brand_in_copy=false` data, ≥2-phrase compliance floor, idempotent. Wired into BOTH generator paths (buildItemHighlights LLM + fallback returns) with the PO's locked title threaded as `PipelineInput.lockedTitle`, and into the regenerate-item-highlight route (whose `recommended_title` IS the locked title). HARDENED 2026-08-08 (adversarial review): (a) the brand-INSERTION row is resolved from SPEC-TRUTH sources only — titles/productType/SKU style numbers, NEVER a search keyphrase — so a pool-contaminated "comfort colors" phrase can no longer stamp a false CC claim on a Gildan family (`resolveBlankRowForNet`, the ONE out-of-pipeline resolver); (b) the waterfall is satisfied only when EVERY shipped title (broadcast + per_child_titles) carries the brand; (c) the carry test is separator-blind ("Comfort-Colors" counts); (d) a title-only section regen re-nets the STORED IH against the new title, and the persist-time lock guard re-nets the fresh IH against the KEPT manual title (`applyBlankBrandNetToDetails` at both write-path sites); (e) every invocation emits a `BLANK_BRAND_NET` JSON decision line. DATA RULE: any new blank_specs row for a never-in-copy brand (every Gildan-family variant) MUST set brand_in_copy=false explicitly — NULL materializes as allowed. KNOWN GAP (out of GO'd scope): the lock-title route does not re-net the ALREADY-STORED IH at lock time — a lock that removes the brand from the title heals on the next IH regen/full regen, not instantly. STICKY-DETAILS PRECEDENCE (ruling 2026-08-08, adversarial review): the waterfall OUTRANKS the sticky-details standing approval — when a sticky-kept PO-ACCEPTED Item Highlight lacks the blank brand while the shipping titles also lack it, the net MAY rewrite the accepted string (this MUST is the newer standing rule, not LLM churn). The rewrite changes recommended_value only (current_value keeps the accepted/live value), so it surfaces as a fresh Push proposal requiring PO action — never ships silently — and the netted row is stamped value_source='spec' (blank_specs-derived) so the next regen's sticky gate reads it as a legitimate spec re-propose instead of re-running the snap→net cycle; every rewrite logs a `BLANK_BRAND_NET decision:'details-rewrite'` line.

## 6. BANNED & PROTECTED TERMS (all published fields + research seeds)
- **"forrest frank" — SCRUB (PO ruling 2026-08-08)**: real recording artist; despite genuine search demand it joins the celebrity/proper-noun scrub class (#102) — never in generated copy or backend. IMPLEMENTED 2026-08-09: phrase-aware `scrubCelebrityNames` runs at the generation exit (`scrubPublished` — title/bullets/description/backend + per-child twins, beside `scrubTrademarks`) AND at the push boundary, and `hasCelebrityName` gates the TITLE_MONEY_TAIL keyword derivation — so stored bytes ≡ pushed bytes and the name can never be welded into a title. NOT YET SHIPPED: ratcheting the stored keyword rows themselves to IRRELEVANT (relevance-classifier/target-set side) — open under tasks #72/#104.
- **Trademarks**: "World Cup"→"World Futbol Cup" (futbol per PO; keeps "cup"); "Super Bowl"→"Big Game"; FIFA/Olympics/NFL/NBA/MLB/NHL/NCAA dropped outright; ~60 team/college/franchise word-marks dropped from the pool (no "for [Brand]" framing exists for them). Generic descriptors (spain, jersey, football, cup) are NOT trademarks — never drop them.
- **Living people**: never ship a person's name (athletes, musicians, actors) — curated conservative list, enforced at generation AND push.
- **Competitor blanks** (Gildan, Bella+Canvas, Hanes, …): never in copy, never in backend — own blank exempted.
- **Competitor retail brands** (Nike, Adidas, Grunt Style, …): off-niche everywhere.
- **Off-niche classes**: wholesale/blank intent, activewear terms on casual tees, foreign-language terms, equipment nouns ("golf tees" the peg), wrong garment cut, dated public events (USA-250/semiquincentennial on non-patriotic designs — a public event is not the wearer's anniversary).
- **Soccer identity**: soccer ≡ football ≡ futbol (asymmetric: soccer→football yes, football→soccer never). **REQUIRED, not merely allowed (PO ruling 2026-08-09, §3 gold rule 3)**: a soccer design must SURFACE "football" as a title/backend candidate. `IDENTITY_SYNONYMS` (src/lib/keyword-engine/keywordResearcher.ts) already guaranteed the bare token entered the pool — but the title money-tail selector only considers 3-5 word candidates carrying a garment noun, so a one-word row could never win the slot the PO's gold spends on it. `identitySynonymPhrases` extends the same map: it MIRRORS phrases the market already returned ("usa mexico canada soccer tee" → "… football tee"), substituting only where the source token directly modifies a garment noun, and each mirror inherits its source row's real opportunity — so the selector still picks on merit, never from a hardcoded string. The adjacency rule is also what keeps "world soccer cup" (our own trademark substitution) from becoming "world football cup".

## 7. CUSTOMIZATION (Amazon Custom)
- Amazon does NOT expose enrollment in the Listings API (probed live) → **customizable is a seller declaration** (portal flag; toggle UI in progress).
- When TRUE: "**Personalized**" LEADS the title facts, personalization language is encouraged in bullets/description ("with your own text"), `personalized custom` join backend. Both stem families used deliberately (Personalized… + Customize…).
- When FALSE (the catalog default): the words are banned — a false claim is worse than a short title.

## 8. BULLETS · DESCRIPTION · ITEM HIGHLIGHTS
- **Bullets**: exactly 5, each **150–200 chars**: `ALL-CAPS 2-3 WORD BENEFIT HOOK - one complete sentence.` Hook is a real benefit, never a label; never starts with a dash; garment brand named once (when it's a selling point); design name woven in; quality over coverage — clean 776 beats stuffed 900.
- **Description**: **900–980 visible chars**, HTML with `<b>` + `<ul><li>`, grounded in real facts (fabric/weight/dye/fit/neck/sleeve) + design story + styling; no search-phrase prose, no brand in body, no internal jargon (seller/SKU/ASIN/listing/keyword/backend).
- **Item Highlights** (title_differentiation): ≤75 chars, 2–3 short benefit PHRASES (no sentences), customer-facing (never a keyword list), adds what the title lacks, no word repeated >2×, no promo language. Written as COPY, never a bare fact-join (PO 2026-08-04: "Cotton tee, Crew Neck, for women" class rejected) — each fact pairs with a feel/benefit word; no title-echo.
- **UNISEX FIT CLARITY (PO 2026-08-06)**: the whole current catalog is UNISEX blanks marketed to WOMEN. The listings MUST make the unisex sizing explicit — but ONLY in Bullets, Description, and Features/Item Highlights. **NEVER in the Title** ("it should not be stuffed to TITLE"). Department stays Womens / target_gender female (the targeting is the strategy; the copy sets the fit expectation). Standard phrasing: "Unisex sizing runs relaxed — many women size down one for a fitted look." Driven by `blank_specs.unisex` (DB flag; migration 054) → deterministic guarantees in `applyTerminalNets` (bullets clause + description note) + IH fact. Fit-to-Size sentiment stays spec-truth (unchanged by this rule). **The "NEVER in the Title" half is now ENFORCED (2026-08-09), not just asked for**: `stripTitleWasteVocabulary` removes "Unisex" (and "Classic Fit", §3 gold rule 4) from the shipped title bytes at the `bandTitle` seam, under the PO's guard — only when the removal frees space for a money keyword or the title still lands 70-75. TITLE-only: these three fields keep the facts.
- **Product Detail attributes**: values ground to the spec table where a fact exists (Fit, Sleeve, Stretch binary+graded, Fit-to-Size, Collar: Crew Neck → "Round Collar" — the enum has no Crew member). **Model Name carries NO brand** (PO 2026-08-04): the Brand attribute already says it; "THE CEO Cupid Valentine" → "Cupid Valentine" (deterministic strip of seller + garment brand).

## 9. MULTI-DESIGN FAMILIES
- Each design group gets its OWN title/bullets/description; content anchors on the DESIGN (vision-read artwork), never the shirt color.
- **Couple sets** (two halves of one concept, e.g. Rude Potato + Sweet Potato): ONE shared title naming both + the concept, shared copy broadcast — strict detection so friend-group/family packs keep per-design content.
- A child ASIN always resolves to its parent — the family is the working unit.

## 10. SHIPPING & OPERATIONS
- **Nothing writes to live Amazon without explicit PO GO** — the PO personally clicks Ship. An approved plan is standing authorization for its phases; ship actions are the reserved stop.
- Pushes go to **BOTH FBA and FBM twin SKUs** per ASIN (+ parent SKU for titles); push is **UPDATE-ONLY** (never PATCH an offerless SKU — phantom-listing risk); all pushes route through the serial durable queue with the global 5-req/s bucket.
- **Verification is automatic** (PO verbatim 2026-06-13): cron verifies every push against live Amazon, re-ships until 100%, notifies only on major flags.
- **Initial Ship = standing approval for that field** (PO 2026-08-08: "it was always required that the system completes this in a loop automatically so user does not see anything incomplete after initial push"): once the PO has shipped a core field for a family, later regen updates to that field's recommendation auto-flow to Amazon (content-reconcile loop) — the PO's review gate applies to the FIRST push of a field, and to fields never shipped before. Delivery-retry (same content) and content-reconcile (updated content, previously-approved field) are distinct loops; both are bounded, loud-logged, and kill-switchable.
- A shipped section **locks for 7 days** ("settling") so scores can't nag; per-section override exists.
- Behavior changes ship **off → shadow → on** with live-regen evidence gates; scorer re-bases are announced, and the PO accepts truth-fix score movement (the 72→70 dip is a rubric fix signal, not a content problem).
- Jungle Scout budget: **950 billable calls/month** self-cap (50 under plan).

## 11. QUALITY PRINCIPLES (anti-Goodhart)
- Terminal nets pad **only from product facts** — never pool terms, never invented adjectives. A number made green with junk is a failure.
- The scorer never reads the ship door's violations; a rising "repaired" count is a producer regression signal.
- Preserve-prior fires only when the fresh output is empty/unparseable OR strictly worse — and a contaminated prior is never "better".
- Honest claims: the optimizer controls **indexing/relevance only** — "indexed for", never "will rank #1".
- The PO's qualitative gate: padded copy must read like a product, not filler — the PO reads samples and the read outranks the numbers.
