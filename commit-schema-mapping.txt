THE BUG (PO: sticky notes recommended as "...T-Shirt, Graphic Tee for Men and Women"):
ai-recommendations hardcoded category 'Clothing, Shoes & Jewelry > ... > Novelty' for EVERY
product, and looksApparel() text-sniffs category+title (\bclothing\b matches) -> every product
was apparel unless rescued by a second hardcoded noun list (memory cards, mugs...) that did not
know "sticky notes". Two stacked hardcodes; SELF_STICK_NOTE got the full apparel treatment in
title, bullets, and description.

THE FIX (no new hardcodes -- the live SP-API productType decides):
- route: resolve productType ONCE before the pipeline (was fetched only AFTER, for enum
  validation); pass it into PipelineInput; prompt category becomes the humanized PT
  ("Self Stick Note") instead of the clothing path. PT lookup failure -> null -> byte-identical
  legacy behavior (fail-open).
- listingPipeline: looksApparel(category, repTitle, productType?) -- when PT is known it is
  AUTHORITATIVE both directions (APPAREL_PRODUCT_TYPES token-boundary regex: SHIRT, SWEATSHIRT,
  DRESS, ...). Kills the poisoned-category bug AND the feedback loop where a previously
  contaminated live title ("...T-Shirt" pushed onto an office product) kept flipping it back to
  apparel forever. All 8 call sites pass it.
