Background-friendly pushes: close the modal and keep working (floating progress pill)

PO: "Why can't I close it? an employee needs to watch an item send for 5 min before working
on another section." Root insight: the push stream lives in the PAGE's JavaScript, not the
modal - the modal was never load-bearing. Closing it is safe; only closing the browser TAB
kills the stream.

- Push + Auto Push modals now close freely during a send (overlay click, X, "Hide (keeps
  running)"). The employee works on other sections - or other listings via in-app navigation -
  while the stream finishes.
- Floating progress pill (bottom-right) whenever a push runs with its modal closed:
  "Pushing Fit Type... 34 accepted - view" / "Auto Push running... 3/7 fields" -> click
  reopens the modal mid-stream.
- beforeunload guard while any push runs: the browser confirms before tab close/refresh -
  the one action that genuinely interrupts (already-accepted SKUs stay pushed; Verify ->
  "Push just the stale" recovers).
- In-modal note updated to the new semantics.

Known v1 limit (stated in PR): the pill renders on the listing page that started the push -
navigating to another page keeps the push running but without the pill; Verify on return
shows the result. True server-side background jobs (survive tab close + deploys) are the
follow-up option if wanted.
